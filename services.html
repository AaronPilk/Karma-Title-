What property type requires title insurance?

Title insurance carries even greater significance in commercial real estate due to the typically elevated purchase prices and intricate purchase agreements involved.

Why do I need commercial title insurance?

Commercial title insurance safeguards the investor in case any complications arise concerning the property's title — from negligence and clerical errors to fraud.

Types of policies and who pays

The lender's policy remains valid for the duration of the loan, whereas the owner's policy provides coverage for as long as the individual owns the property.
